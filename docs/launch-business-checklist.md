# Before public launch: business information and documents

Everything here is **yours to provide or decide**. The website can be deployed and tested without them, but it should **not be announced publicly** until the items marked **Required** are done.

This is a practical checklist, not legal advice. For the legal items, a UK solicitor or a reputable template service is recommended.

## 1. Company identity (shown in the footer)

- [x] Registered legal entity name: Reduium Shipment Ltd (on the site; confirm it matches Companies House exactly)
- [ ] **Required:** Company registration number (hidden on the site until provided)
- [ ] Registered office address, if different from the head office (115 Crown Street, London)
- [ ] Postcode for the head office address (recommended)
- [ ] VAT number, if VAT-registered

## 2. Contact and support

- [x] Support email: info@reduiumshipment.com (on the site)
- [ ] **Required:** The info@reduiumshipment.com mailbox actually exists and someone reads it
- [x] Support phone: +44 113 320 7332 (on the site)
- [ ] **Required:** The phone line is answered 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, as the site now states (or has voicemail outside staffed hours)
- [x] Support hours: 24 hours a day, 7 days a week (on the site)
- [ ] Who answers support requests in the admin dashboard, and how quickly you aim to reply

## 3. Legal pages and data protection (UK)

- [ ] **Required:** **Privacy notice**: what personal data you collect (contact-form messages; sender/recipient details on shipments), why, how long you keep it, who sees it, and how people can ask for a copy or deletion
- [ ] **Required:** **Terms** for using the website / tracking service, and your terms or conditions of carriage if you publish them
- [ ] **Required:** **ICO registration**: most UK organisations that handle personal data must pay the annual data protection fee to the Information Commissioner's Office (check at ico.org.uk)
- [ ] **Required:** **Retention policy**: how long support messages, shipment records and backups are kept, and who deletes them (the site has admin-only permanent deletion)
- [ ] Cookie statement: the site sets only a sign-in cookie for staff and remembers the visitor's chosen language on their device; no tracking or advertising cookies. Confirm the wording with your adviser
- [ ] Who in the company is responsible for data protection questions

## 4. Services and content accuracy

- [ ] **Required:** The four services on the site (Express Parcel, Air Freight, Ocean Freight, Road & Ground) match what Reduium really offers. Remove or rename any that don't
- [ ] Any claims on the site (customs handling, coverage, "a scan at every facility") are accurate for your operation
- [ ] Countries/regions you ship to (if you want this stated)
- [ ] Any licences, registrations or insurance relevant to carrying goods (e.g. customs/AEO status), if you plan to mention them

## 5. Brand and images

- [ ] **Required:** **Licences for the three homepage photographs**: proof you may use them commercially (source, licence type, any credit required). Replace any you can't verify
- [ ] Logo: confirm it's yours to use; consider checking the name/trademark at the UK IPO

## 6. People and access

- [ ] **Required:** Who will be the first **Administrator** (creates the account personally at launch)
- [ ] Which staff need **Operator** accounts
- [ ] Every staff member uses a strong, unique password (two-factor sign-in is deferred)
- [ ] Who owns and pays for the hosting, database, backup storage, domain and email accounts (use company details and a shared/company email where possible, not a personal one)
- [ ] Two-step sign-in enabled on those provider accounts (registrar, host, Neon, email, GitHub)

## 7. Translations

- [ ] For each language you want to rely on, a **fluent/native speaker review**: Spanish, French, German, Portuguese (Brazil), Chinese (Simplified), Arabic, Japanese
- [ ] After a review, the language can be marked `reviewed` (then it's also chosen automatically for visitors whose browser uses that language). Until then it stays `draft`, available only by choice, with English as the automatic default

## 8. Launch decisions

- [ ] Launch date
- [ ] Whether to keep the site hidden from search engines until launch (recommended)
- [ ] Monthly hosting budget approved (see the deployment plan, §1)
